Smaruf develops free book-related mobile applications. These apps are provided at no cost and are intended for use as-is. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled across all of our applications.

Information Collection and Use

Our applications do not require you to create an account and do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number directly.

However, our apps use third-party services that automatically collect certain data, including device or other identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and app usage/diagnostic information. This data is used to deliver advertisements and to analyze app performance.

Data We Collect and Share

  • Device or other IDs (e.g. Advertising ID) — collected and shared with advertising/analytics providers.
  • App activity & diagnostics — crash logs and usage statistics.
  • Approximate location — derived from IP address for ad delivery.

Third-Party Services

Our apps use the following Google services, which may collect and process the data listed above:

  • Google AdMob — for showing advertisements. Ads policy
  • Google Firebase / Analytics — for analytics and crash reporting. Firebase privacy
  • Google Play Services — Google Privacy Policy

Permissions Used

  • INTERNET — to load advertisements or online content.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check network connectivity.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — for advertising (personalized or non-personalized ads).

Data Sharing

Device identifiers and usage data may be shared with the third-party providers listed above (Google AdMob, Firebase) solely to provide advertising and analytics services. We do not sell your data.

Data Security

The developer does not store user data on its own servers. Any data processed by third-party services is handled securely under their respective privacy policies. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
