Summary

Yoqimtoy Bo‘lishning 6 Qoidasi is a reading application with a book (6 parts) about human relations and the etiquette of conversation, in Uzbek. The application does not require registration or a user account. The developer does not operate servers that receive or store user data. Reading progress and settings are stored locally on the device. The application is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

Information stored on your device

The application may store the following information locally:

  • Reading progress: how far you have read in each part and the last part you read.
  • Application settings: dark mode, text size and the reading background (white, sepia or night).

This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.
