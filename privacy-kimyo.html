Summary

Kimyo Ilmi is a chemistry learning application: an interactive periodic table of the chemical elements, short lessons with exercises, a molar mass calculator, element records and rankings, and a short history of the periodic law. It has no user accounts, no sign-up and no login, and it does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files. Your lesson progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any server.

The application is free and shows ads. It uses the internet only for ads, for checking the application's status and for updates (through Google Play), and for loading the “Our other projects” list.

Information the app stores on your device

The following information is stored locally, in storage that only this application can read, and is not sent to us:

  • Learning progress: which lessons you have completed, your results in lesson exercises and in the periodic table questions.
  • Application settings: language, dark mode and display options.

Information the app itself does NOT collect

The application does not collect, request or access: name, email address, phone number or any account identifier; location or GPS data; contacts, SMS messages, call history, photos, camera or microphone; health or medical data; and it contains no analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting of its own. We do not read your progress or settings and have no access to them. Third-party advertising services are described separately below.

Network requests

The application uses the internet to show ads, to check its status and version on our server (versionapps.magistrx.uz) and to load the “Our other projects” list from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). The application may also ask Google Play whether an update is available. These requests do not contain your progress, settings or personal information such as your name or email.

Advertising and third-party services

The application uses the following third-party services, which may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): to show ads and request consent. policies.google.com/privacy, policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Yandex Mobile Ads (only through AdMob mediation): the application does not run Yandex advertising on its own. When AdMob chooses the Yandex ad network to fill an ad slot, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK bundled in the application loads and measures that ad and may collect data under Yandex's privacy policy. yandex.com/legal/privacy

These services may collect the Advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, and ad interactions (views, clicks). They use this to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you consent, personalise ads. We do not have access to this data and do not collect it ourselves.

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the application shows a Google UMP consent form. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy choices.

Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to load ads, check the application's status and know whether the internet is available.
  • AD_ID (advertising ID): for the ad providers above.

The application does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notifications. Advertising libraries may declare additional technical permissions needed to operate; these do not give access to your personal data.

Sharing

We do not sell or share the progress and settings stored on your device with anyone, because they never leave it. If you use the “Share the app” button, the application link is passed through your device's own share sheet to the app you choose; we do not receive it. Links in the application (for example, Telegram or the developer's page on Google Play) open an external app or browser, which have their own privacy policies.

Backup

If Android's automatic backup is enabled on your phone, the application's data may be backed up to your own Google account and restored when you set up a new device. This is a feature of the Android operating system; the backup belongs to your Google account and the developer has no access to it. You can disable it in your device's Google backup settings.

Data retention and deletion

Your progress and settings stay on your device until you delete them. To delete everything, uninstall the application or clear its data in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Kimyo Ilmi → Storage → Clear data). Since there is no account and no server-side profile, no copy of your data remains with us. For data collected by ad providers, please use their privacy pages listed above.

Security

Your data is stored in the application's private storage area, which the Android operating system isolates from other applications. The application's own network requests use HTTPS.

Children

The application is not directed at children under 13. It does not contain content designed to appeal to children and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us.

Кратко

Kimyo Ilmi — приложение для изучения химии: интерактивная периодическая таблица химических элементов, короткие уроки с упражнениями, калькулятор молярной массы, рекорды и рейтинги элементов и краткая история периодического закона. В нём нет учётных записей, регистрации и входа, оно не запрашивает имя, email, номер телефона, местоположение, контакты, фотографии или файлы. Ваши результаты обучения и настройки хранятся только на вашем устройстве и никогда не отправляются ни нам, ни на какой-либо сервер.
