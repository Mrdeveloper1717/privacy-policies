Biznes Maslahatlari ilovasi (com.sadirboyprogrammer.savdogar)
Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 10-noyabr

Ushbu siyosat «Biznes Maslahatlari» Android ilovasi (keyingi o'rinlarda «Ilova») sizning ma'lumotlaringiz bilan qanday ishlashini tushuntiradi. Ilovani ishlab chiqaruvchi: Kitoblar Olami | Smaruf («Biz»).

1. Qisqacha

  • Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish, hisob yaratish yoki kirish yo'q.
  • Biz sizning ismingiz, telefon raqamingiz, elektron pochtangiz, joylashuvingiz, kontaktlaringiz, rasmlaringiz yoki fayllaringizni so'ramaymiz va yig'maymiz.
  • Maslahatlar matni ilova ichida joylashgan va internetsiz o'qiladi.
  • Ilovada reklama ko'rsatiladi. Reklama xizmatlari (Google AdMob va Yandex Ads) o'z maqsadlari uchun qurilma ma'lumotlarini ishlatishi mumkin. Bu haqida 4-bo'limda yozilgan.

2. Qurilmangizda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

Quyidagi sozlamalar faqat qurilmangiz xotirasida saqlanadi va bizga yuborilmaydi:

  • ilova tili (lotin, kirill, ingliz) va yozuv sozlamalari;
  • matn o'lchami, shrift, mavzu va qorong'i rejim tanlovi;
  • oxirgi o'qilgan sahifa va sahifadagi joy;
  • ilova funksiyalari haqidagi maslahat oynasi ko'rsatilgani va «Boshqa ilovalarimiz» bo'limidagi ilovalarning ko'rilgani haqidagi belgilar.

Ilovani o'chirib tashlasangiz, bu ma'lumotlar ham o'chadi.

3. Internet orqali yuboriladigan so'rovlar

Ilova ishlashi uchun quyidagi tarmoq so'rovlarini yuboradi. Ularda sizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringiz (ism, pochta va h.k.) yuborilmaydi, lekin har qanday internet so'rovidagidek qurilmangizning IP manzili va texnik ma'lumotlar (so'rov vaqti, ilova paketi nomi) server tomoniga ko'rinadi:

  • Ilova holati va versiyasi tekshiruvi: versionapps.magistrx.uz serveridan ilovaning joriy holati (masalan, yangilanish talab qilinishi) olinadi. Server oddiy texnik jurnallarni (IP, vaqt, so'rov manzili) xavfsizlik va nosozliklarni aniqlash uchun saqlashi mumkin. Biz bu jurnallarni reklama yoki profil yaratish uchun ishlatmaymiz.
  • «Boshqa ilovalarimiz» ro'yxati: ilovalar ro'yxati GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) orqali yuklanadi.
  • Reklama: 4-bo'limga qarang.

4. Reklama va uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

Ilova quyidagi uchinchi tomon xizmatlaridan foydalanadi. Ular o'z maxfiylik siyosatlariga muvofiq ma'lumot to'plashi mumkin:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK va Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): reklama ko'rsatish va rozilik so'rash uchun. policies.google.com/privacy, policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Yandex Mobile Ads: reklama ko'rsatish uchun. yandex.com/legal/privacy

Bu xizmatlar reklama identifikatori (Advertising ID), IP manzil, qurilma turi va operatsion tizimi, ilova ichidagi reklama bilan o'zaro ta'sir (ko'rish, bosish) kabi ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi mumkin. Ular reklamani ko'rsatish, o'lchash, soxtalikni oldini olish va (rozilik bo'lsa) shaxsiylashtirish uchun ishlatiladi. Biz bu ma'lumotlarga kirish huquqiga ega emasmiz va ularni o'zimiz yig'maymiz.

Rozilik: Yevropa Iqtisodiy Hududi, Buyuk Britaniya va shunga o'xshash hududlarda ilova Google UMP orqali rozilik oynasini ko'rsatadi. Tanlovingizni keyinroq Sozlamalar → Maxfiylik tanlovlari bo'limidan o'zgartirishingiz mumkin.

Reklama identifikatori: Android sozlamalarida (Sozlamalar → Google → Reklamalar) reklama identifikatorini tiklash yoki shaxsiylashtirilgan reklamadan voz kechishingiz mumkin.

5. Ruxsatlar

  • INTERNET va ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: reklama va ilova holatini tekshirish uchun internet borligini bilish.
  • AD_ID (reklama identifikatori): reklama xizmatlari uchun.

Ilova kamera, mikrofon, joylashuv, kontaktlar yoki xotiraga kirish ruxsatini so'ramaydi.

6. Ulashish va tashqi havolalar

Matnni ulashish tugmasini bossangiz, tanlangan matn qurilmangizning o'zidagi «Ulashish» oynasi orqali siz tanlagan ilovaga yuboriladi. Biz uni olmaymiz. Ilovadagi Telegram va boshqa havolalar tashqi ilova yoki brauzerni ochadi; ularning maxfiylik siyosati alohida.

7. Bolalar

Ilova 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va biz ularning shaxsiy ma'lumotlarini ataylab yig'maymiz. Agar bola ma'lumot yuborgan deb hisoblasangiz, biz bilan bog'laning.

8. Ma'lumotlarni saqlash va o'chirish

Biz sizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni saqlamaganimiz sababli, o'chirish uchun hech narsa talab etilmaydi. Qurilmadagi sozlamalarni ilovani o'chirish yoki Android sozlamalarida ilova ma'lumotlarini tozalash orqali o'chirishingiz mumkin. Reklama xizmatlari yig'gan ma'lumotlar bo'yicha so'rovlar uchun ularning maxfiylik sahifalariga murojaat qiling.

9. Xavfsizlik

Barcha tarmoq so'rovlari HTTPS orqali yuboriladi. Internet orqali hech qanday ma'lumotni 100% himoyalab bo'lmasligini unutmang.

10. Siyosatdagi o'zgarishlar

Siyosat vaqti-vaqti bilan yangilanishi mumkin. Yangi versiya shu sahifada e'lon qilinadi va yuqoridagi «kuchga kirgan sana» yangilanadi.

Privacy Policy

Biznes Maslahatlari (Business Tips) app (com.sadirboyprogrammer.savdogar)
Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the “Biznes Maslahatlari” Android app (the “App”) handles your information. The App is developed by Kitoblar Olami | Smaruf (“we”).

1. Summary

  • The App has no sign-up, no accounts and no login.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • The business tips are bundled inside the App and can be read offline.
  • The App shows ads. Ad providers (Google AdMob and Yandex Ads) may use device data for their own purposes (see section 4).

2. Data stored on your device

These settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us:

  • App language (Latin, Cyrillic, English) and script settings;
  • text size, font, theme and dark mode choices;
  • last read page and position on the page;
  • flags showing that the tips dialog was displayed and that apps in the “Our other projects” section were viewed.

Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

3. Network requests

To work, the App makes the requests below. They do not include personal data such as your name or email, but, as with any internet request, your IP address and technical details (request time, the App's package name) are visible to the receiving server:

  • App status / version check: the App fetches its current status (for example, whether an update is required) from versionapps.magistrx.uz. The server may keep standard technical logs (IP, time, requested URL) for security and troubleshooting. We do not use them for advertising or profiling.
  • “Our other projects” list: loaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
  • Advertising: see section 4.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): to show ads and request consent. policies.google.com/privacy, policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Yandex Mobile Ads: to show ads. yandex.com/legal/privacy

These services may collect the Advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, and ad interactions (views, clicks). They use this to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you consent, personalise ads. We do not have access to this data and do not collect it ourselves.

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the App shows a Google UMP consent form. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy choices.

Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to know whether the internet is available for ads and the status check.
  • AD_ID (advertising ID): for ad providers.

The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage.

6. Sharing and external links

If you use the share button, the selected text is passed through your device's own share sheet to the app you choose. We do not receive it. Links in the App (such as Telegram) open an external app or browser, which have their own privacy policies.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us.

8. Retention and deletion

Because we do not store your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete. You can remove on-device settings by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android settings. For data collected by ad providers, please use their privacy pages.

9. Security

All network requests use HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated.
